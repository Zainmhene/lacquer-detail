Questions

Before youbook.

Will the price go up once you start?

No. After the free check you get a fixed quote, and that's what you pay. If we spot anything else along the way, we call you first, and nothing gets added without your OK.

My car has broken down. Can you collect it?

Yes. Tick "We collect it" when you book (+$35) and we'll pick it up. Breakdown pickup runs 24/7.

How long will you have my car?

A diagnostic usually takes about 45 minutes, and most repairs are done the same day. If a part has to be ordered in, we'll tell you how long before you agree to anything.

What if the problem comes back?

Every repair has a 12-month parts and labour warranty. If something we fixed fails within a year, bring it back and we'll sort it.
